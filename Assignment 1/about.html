Our Story

Bringing Artisan Coffee to Our Hometown

Guar Chempedak Coffee Roasters was founded on a simple idea: everyone deserves a truly great cup of coffee. We saw the amazing coffee chains in big cities and asked, "Why not here?"

We started in a small garage, tinkering with a second-hand roaster, obsessed with finding the perfect roast profile. Our mission is to blend world-class coffee techniques with the warm, welcoming spirit of our local community.

Today, we're proud to be a hub for coffee lovers, students, and families. We believe coffee connects people, and we're honored to be a part of your daily life.
